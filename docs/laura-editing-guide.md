# Editing your website text

You can update the words on your homepage yourself — no code, no waiting.

## How to open the editor

1. Go to **https://divineemergence.org/admin**
2. Click **Login with GitHub** and sign in.
   (One time only: your developer will send you a GitHub invite to accept first.)

## How to make a change

1. On the left you'll see the homepage sections: **Hero, About Laura, Breathwork,
   Retreat, Mission, Closing call-to-action.** Click the one you want to change.
2. You'll see labeled boxes like *Headline*, *Intro paragraph*, *Button label*.
   Edit the text right in the boxes.
   - To add a paragraph or a fact, click the **+** button in a list.
   - To reorder items, drag them by the handle.
3. When you're happy, click **Save** (top left). This does *not* go live yet.
4. Open the **Editorial Workflow** tab at the top and drag your change to
   **Ready**, or just tell your developer it's ready.

## What happens next

Your developer gets a notification, previews your change on a test link, and
publishes it. The live site updates a minute or two later. This review step is
there to catch typos or broken formatting — it keeps your site safe.

## Good to know

- You can edit as much as you like; nothing goes live until it's approved.
- If something looks off, don't worry — you can't break the live site from here.
- **To make a word gold**, wrap it in double asterisks: `**like this**`. It will
  show up in the gold accent color on the site. (Single asterisks `*like this*`
  make a word bright white instead.)
- **Button links** are set by your developer. You control all the wording; if you
  need a link changed, just ask.

Questions? Message your developer — that's the whole point of this setup.
